# Design System: YOUPASS × WORD ATLAS 4.0

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Sky-Cerulean & Pure Porcelain 4.0 Academic Studio"**

YOUPASS × WORD ATLAS 4.0 unites the crisp, daylight-first commercial clarity of YouPass 4.0 with the structured bilingual lexical depth of Word-Atlas (`7,298` illustrated words) and My-IELTS (`3,674` dictation keywords, `538` reading synonyms, `179` listening paraphrases, and `100` writing structures). Every study module is a numbered wayfinding gate (`01` through `06`) arranged on a luminous Cloud Ice White (`#F8FBFF`) canvas with Pure Porcelain White (`#FFFFFF`) cards and Soft Sky-Blue (`#F0F7FF` / `#E0F2FE`) focal frames.

Rather than relying on heavy dark-mode slabs or decorative gradients, the system uses Sky-Cerulean (`#0284C7`) commercial actions, crisp `#DCEBFA` 1px structural hairlines, and interactive inline passage dictionary popovers so learners can study for hours in complete visual comfort.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Asymmetric 12-column Bento Studio architecture with numbered gate headers (`01`–`06`) integrated directly into section titles.
- Cloud Ice White daylight ground (`#F8FBFF`) paired with Pure Porcelain White modules (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Sky-Blue focal headers (`#F0F7FF` / `#E0F2FE`), and Sky-Cerulean (`#0284C7`) primary actions.
- Tri-voice typography combining geometric display signage (`Archivo`), native Vietnamese editorial legibility (`Be Vietnam Pro`), and tabular phonetic/telemetry precision (`JetBrains Mono`).
- Semantic color coding across all 6 CEFR levels (`A1`–`C2`), FSRS memory rating intervals (`Again`, `Hard`, `Good`, `Easy`), and `REVEAL: ON / HIDDEN` self-testing states.

## Colors

The palette pairs pure porcelain surfaces with calming sky-blue tonal steps and deep academic navy ink so learners can read long passages, practice dictation, and review flashcards without eye strain.

### Primary
- **Sky-Cerulean Primary** (`#0284C7`): Anchors all primary commercial calls-to-action (`Lộ Trình Cá Nhân`, `Nâng Cấp PRO`, `Chấm Bài AI`), active tab states, active constellation nodes, and FSRS `Good` ratings. Hover shifts to Deep Cerulean (`#0369A1`).
- **Sky-Azure Accent** (`#0EA5E9`): Highlights interactive links and focus rings.

### Secondary
- **Emerald Mastery** (`#059669` & `#047857`): Confirms verified dictation answers, `Easy` FSRS retention mastery, active `REVEAL: ON` self-test pills, and saved vocabulary notebook states on `#ECFDF5` / `#A7F3D0` surfaces.

### Tertiary
- **Amber Timer & Part-of-Speech** (`#D97706` & `#B45309`): Marks live exam countdown clocks, `Hard` FSRS review intervals, and part-of-speech labels on `#FFFBEB` / `#FDE68A` surfaces.
- **Rose Alert** (`#E11D48`): Marks `Again` FSRS reset buttons, critical exam frequency badges, and error states on `#FFF1F2` / `#FECDD3` surfaces.

### Neutral & Sky Surfaces
- **Cloud Ice Ground** (`#F8FBFF`) & **Pure Porcelain White** (`#FFFFFF`): Primary page canvas and Bento module containers.
- **Soft Sky Frost** (`#F0F7FF`) & **Sky Wash** (`#E0F2FE`): Interactive header bars, Station `02 / Hôm Nay Học Gì` hero wash, and inline passage dictionary cards.
- **Sky Hairline Rule** (`#DCEBFA`) & **Sky Focus Border** (`#BAE6FD`): Structural 1px dividers separating rows, tabs, and sub-panels.
- **Academic Navy Ink** (`#0F172A`), **Slate Body Ink** (`#334155`), & **Muted Telemetry Slate** (`#64748B`): High-contrast text hierarchy across all light surfaces.

### Named Rules
**The Unified Sky-Porcelain Rule.** Every page (`/`, `/login`, `/roadmap`, `/pricing`, `/admin`) uses `#F8FBFF` Cloud Ice ground and `#FFFFFF` / `#F0F7FF` surfaces with `#DCEBFA` hairlines — never dark charcoal slabs.

**The Focal Sky Wash Rule.** Soft Sky Wash (`#E0F2FE` / `#F0F7FF`) with `#BAE6FD` borders is reserved for Station `02 / Hôm Nay Học Gì`, active filter headers, and inline passage dictionary cards so the primary learning task stands out immediately.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with system sans-serif fallback)
**Body Font:** Be Vietnam Pro (with sans-serif fallback)
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with monospace fallback)

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900 weight, 2.25rem / 36px, line-height 1.05): Active vocabulary headwords in Station 02 and top masthead identity.
- **Headline** (800 weight, 1.25rem / 20px, line-height 1.2): Section gate headers (`05 / KHO DỮ LIỆU HỌC THUẬT MY-IELTS`) and passage titles.
- **Title** (800 weight, 0.9375rem / 15px, line-height 1.3): Bento module headers (`01 / LỘ TRÌNH ADAPTIVE`, `03 / BẢN ĐỒ 19 CHỦ ĐỀ`, `04 / BẢNG VÀNG & ĐUA TOP`) and primary buttons.
- **Body** (400–500 weight, 1rem / 16px, line-height 1.6, 65–75ch max measure): Vietnamese and English definitions, contextual example sentences, and reading passages.
- **Label** (700 weight, 0.75rem / 12px, line-height 1.2, `tabular-nums`): IPA transcriptions, CEFR chips, FSRS interval buttons (`[1] Again 10m`), and question indices.

### Named Rules
**The Single-Line Gate Header Rule.** Module numbers (`01 /`, `02 /`, `03 /`) are integrated directly into the heading line itself; never place a small uppercase kicker or eyebrow label above a heading.

**The Tabular Telemetry Rule.** Every counter, streak digit, countdown clock, XP score, and FSRS day interval uses `JetBrains Mono` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

## Layout

The layout uses a centered `1440px` maximum container (`px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`) built on a 12-column CSS Grid (`gap-5` / `20px`). In the first viewport, Column 1 (`lg:col-span-3`) holds the Adaptive Route & Personal Vocabulary Notebook, Column 2 (`lg:col-span-6`) gives 50% of the horizontal width to Station `02 / Hôm Nay Học Gì`, and Column 3 (`lg:col-span-3`) stacks the 19-Topic Constellation radar over the Hall of Fame & VIP Leaderboard.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is achieved through crisp `#DCEBFA` 1px borders and subtle Sky-Cerulean ambient elevation shadows (`0 12px 32px -10px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`). Inside Bento modules, sub-regions divide via `1px solid #DCEBFA` hairlines rather than nested drop-shadow cards.

### Named Rules
**The No-Nested-Card Rule.** A Bento module is a single architectural container; sub-sections inside it divide via `1px solid #DCEBFA` hairlines and flush table rows, never boxed cards inside boxed cards.

## Shapes

Top-level Bento modules use `16px` (`rounded-2xl`) outer corners with a `1px solid #DCEBFA` structural border. Inner lexical stages use `12px` (`rounded-xl`), interactive buttons and inputs use `8px` (`rounded-lg`), and compact CEFR/collocation tags use `6px` (`rounded`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sky-Cerulean CTA:** `#0284C7` background with `#FFFFFF` bold text, shifting to `#0369A1` on hover.
- **Secondary Sky Frost Action:** `#F0F7FF` background with `#BAE6FD` border and `#0284C7` text, shifting to `#E0F2FE` on hover.

### Chips
- **Style:** Compact `6px` radius badges in `JetBrains Mono` (`12px`, bold) encoding CEFR levels and collocation phrases (`#F0F7FF` background with `#0284C7` text).

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** `16px` outer radius on primary Bento cells.
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` Pure Porcelain White (standard modules) or `#F0F7FF` → `#FFFFFF` Sky Wash (Hero Station 02).
- **Border:** `1px solid #DCEBFA` (`#BAE6FD` on focal modules).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `1px solid #BAE6FD` stroke on `#F8FBFF` Cloud Ice background with `8px` radius and `#0F172A` text.
- **Focus:** `2px solid #0284C7` focus-visible outline.

### Split-Flap Streak Counter
- **Description:** Mechanical digit tiles (`28px × 36px`) rendered in `JetBrains Mono` 800 weight on `#0284C7` plates with `#FFFFFF` numerals, housed in a `#F0F7FF` bezel with `#BAE6FD` border.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** integrate section numbers (`01 /`, `02 /`, `03 /`) directly into the heading text on a single line.
- **Do** use custom SVG flags and consistent stroke icons (`lucide-react`) for audio, streak, and navigation controls.
- **Do** keep all surfaces in the unified `#F8FBFF` / `#FFFFFF` / `#F0F7FF` Sky-Blue & Pure White 4.0 palette and format all numeric telemetry with `tabular-nums`.
- **Do** separate sub-regions inside Bento panels using `1px solid #DCEBFA` hairlines and flush grid cells.

### Don't:
- **Don't** place small uppercase kicker or eyebrow labels above headings.
- **Don't** use Unicode emojis (`🔥`, `🇬🇧`, `🇺🇸`, `🏆`) anywhere in the interface.
- **Don't** nest boxed cards inside boxed cards or add decorative `linear-gradient` grid backgrounds to the page body.
- **Don't** use gradient text, hard offset neobrutalist shadows, or thick colored `border-left` / `border-bottom` stripes on rounded elements.
